Apply for a residence permit and book an appointment with the Immigration Service

• Apply for a residence permit at https://www.nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply Be aware to choose the application that suits your situation.

• After you have completed the application, book an appointment online to finalize your residence permit at https://nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/Contact-us/Contact-the-Danish-Immigration-Service/Citizen-Service

If you have questions about residency, processing times or need guidance on the rules for residence and work permits in Denmark you can call the Immigration Service at +45 35 36 66 00.

Find a permanent home

At the Municipality of Sønderborg we have different kinds of houses and apartments which you can rent or buy.

If you would like to rent an apartment, you should contact one of our housing organizations (Danish: boligforeninger) and become a member of the housing organization. You will have to pay a small tuition in order to become a member.

You will find further information about rentals, purchasing real estate and other relevant housing issues here: www.sonderborg.dk/en/accommodation

Apply for a tax card and a Danish personal tax number at SKAT

When you live in Denmark you need to have a tax card. SKAT is the Danish tax authority, where you can apply for a tax card and a Danish personal tax number.

You can apply for a tax card here: https://lifeindenmark.borger.dk/money-and-tax

Register at the local Citizen Service (Borgerservice)

When you have finalized the process with the Immigration Service, you will receive your residence permit and can register at Borgerservice in Sønderborg.

Read more about the registration process with Borgerservice: www.sonderborgkommune.dk/en/indrejse When you have been registered at a residence in Sønderborg Municipality, Borgerservice will make sure that you receive the yellow health card (Danish: sundhedskort). This entitles you to healthcare services at a doctor, hospital, etc.

Read more here: https:// lifeindenmark.borger.dk/ healthcare/health-insurance/ health-insurance-card

4

If you have questions regarding your residence permit, you can contact the Immigration Service at +45 35 36 66 00.

If you have questions regarding the registration proces and the yellow health insurance card, you can contact Borgerservice at + 45 88 72 40 06.

Apply for MitID and create an e-Boks

MitID is your personal digital ID and can be used for various purposes, including transferring money in online banking or logging into public self-service solutions like www.skat.dk, www.borger.dk and www.sundhed.dk

How to get MitID: https://lifeindenmark.borger.dk/apps-and-digital-services/mitid

If you have any questions regarding MitID, you can contact Borgerservice at + 45 88 72 40 06.

Create an e-Boks e-Boks is your digital mailbox on the web. In e-Boks you have all your important mail gathered in a safe place where you e.g. receive your pay check from your employer and letters from public institutions.

Register yourself as a user of e-Boks here: https://private.e-boks.com/danmark/en

If you have questions about e-Boks you can find more information here:

https://brugersupport.e-boks.dk/hc/en-us

or contact e-Boks at +45 82 30 32 30.

Register your car at Motorstyrelsen

If you take up residence in Denmark and bring a vehicle you must register your foreign registered vehicle within 30 days of arrival and you must pay a registration tax.

Further information here: www.motorst.dk/en-us/individuals/importing-vehicles or contact Motorstyrelsen at +45 72 22 28 71.

Driver’s license

You can apply for an exchange of your current driving license, if you have normal residence in Denmark.

Further information here: https://lifeindenmark.borger.dk/travel-and-transport/driving-licence/how-to-obtain-a-driving-license-in-denmark

Check if you are eligible to apply for the blue EU health insurance card

The blue EU health insurance card entitles the holder to treatment on the same terms as the citizens of the country they are traveling in.

The blue European health insurance card is normally available to EU/EEA, Swiss and UK citizens, and in special cases to cross-border workers, expats, stateless individuals, refugees or family members of a person covered by Danish health insurance.

If you are eligible, you must use the blue EU health insurance card when you travel to an EU country. You can order the card and read more here: https://lifeindenmark.borger.dk/healthcare/health-insurance/blue-european-health-insurance-card

If you are not eligible for the blue EU health insurance card, you must arrange your own private travel insurance.
