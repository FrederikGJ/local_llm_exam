The Immigration Service Application Form Guide 27 August 2026 This guide can help you, if you wish to submit an application to the Danish Immigration Service. Use this guide if your application concerns visa (short stay), family reunification, work as religious worker, temporary extension or permanent residence. The guide can help you find the form which best matches your situation. The guide is divided into four groups: • Form group 1: If you wish to apply for a visa (short term). • Form group 2: If you are first time applicant for a Danish residence permit. • Form group 3: If you wish to apply for an extension of your existing residence permit. • Form group 4: If you wish to apply for a permanent residence permit. Please note: it is mandatory to use digital (online) self-service for certain types of applications. For other types of applications it is mandatory to use printable application forms. Unless you are exempt from this requirement. Mandatory online solutions and forms are marked with a red star (*) in the guide. Click on the title of a form or online solution to download the form (pdf) or go directly to the online solution at www.newtodenmark.dk You can read more about the conditions for residence permits at www.newtodenmark.dk.
